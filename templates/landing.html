What it does

Built for the
stockroom floor

Made for electronics manufacturing, where a single missing part can stall a whole build.

01Inventory

Live stock

Every stock, pick, and restock updates counts immediately and lands in the transaction log.

02Planning

Shortage reports

Load a BOM and see what's missing for the build quantity, then export the list for purchasing.

03Access

Roles and audit trail

Five permission levels, including ITAR, plus a full record of who touched what and when.

04Production

BOMs and jobs

Drop in an Excel BOM and columns are detected automatically. Track revisions and build quantities per job.

05Compliance

Moisture and date codes

MSL levels, date codes, and shelf life tracked on every part, with expiring stock flagged early.

06Labeling

Barcode labels

Generate PCNs and print ZPL labels straight to the printer. Scan them from any page.
